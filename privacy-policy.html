Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Money Mechanics collects when you visit moneymechanics.example, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and fund an independent educational website.

Information we collect

When you browse the site, our web server and hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and the time of your visit. We use this information to keep the site secure and working properly.

If you contact us by email through the address listed on our contact page, we receive the information you choose to send, and we use it only to respond to you.

We do not require you to create an account to read our guides, and we do not sell personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We and our advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to measure site traffic, remember certain preferences, and show advertisements. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and to other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to learn about and opt out of certain third-party personalized advertising. Opting out does not stop ads from appearing; it changes how they are selected.

We do not control the content of third-party ads. Please review the privacy policies of the advertisers you interact with.

Analytics

We may use aggregated analytics tools to understand which guides are most useful and to improve navigation. These tools report general usage patterns and do not identify you personally.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to official government sources and other websites, including IRS.gov, SEC.gov, FDIC.gov, and TreasuryDirect.gov. These sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser, opt out of personalized advertising through the links above, and contact us to ask about information we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the advertising technology we use changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.

This policy is provided for general information and is not legal advice. Before launch, review it with a qualified attorney and confirm it matches your actual analytics and advertising setup.
