About Money Mechanics

Independent since 2024 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Money Mechanics publishes plain-English guides to personal finance. We explain how budgeting, saving, investing, taxes, credit, and retirement accounts actually work, so you can make your own decisions with better information.

What we do

Our guides cover everyday money questions: how to build a budget that survives real life, where to keep an emergency fund, how Roth and traditional retirement accounts differ, how credit scores are calculated, and how to plan for major expenses. Each guide explains the mechanics first, then shows worked examples, and then describes the situations where reasonable people disagree.

How we make money

Money Mechanics earns revenue from display advertising, including Google AdSense. We do not sell financial products, manage client assets, accept referral fees, or receive commissions from banks, brokerages, or insurers for recommending their services. If that ever changes, we will disclose it clearly on the affected pages.

Who writes the guides

Articles are written by the Money Mechanics Editorial Team. We work from primary sources such as IRS publications, SEC and FDIC materials, Federal Reserve data, and official plan documents. Our process is described in full in our editorial policy.

What we are not

We are not a registered investment advisor, broker-dealer, bank, or tax preparer. Our content is educational. It does not take into account your personal circumstances, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional who knows your full situation.
